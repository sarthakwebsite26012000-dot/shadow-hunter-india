# Shadow Hunter India 🎮

A stealth-action game inspired by Indian historical settings with top-down gameplay, tap-to-move controls, and original art & code.

## 🎯 Play Now

**Live Game:** https://sarthakwebsite26012000-dot.github.io/shadow-hunter-india/

## 🎮 Game Features

- **Top-down stealth gameplay** - Navigate through Indian-themed environments
- **Tap/click to move** - Simple, intuitive controls for all devices
- **Enemy AI with vision cones** - Avoid detection and eliminate targets
- **Gem collection** - Gather all gems while clearing each level
- **Multiple levels** - Increasing difficulty with more enemies and obstacles
- **Indian historical theme** - Rajasthan forts, palaces, and traditional settings

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Click anywhere on the game canvas to move your character
2. Approach enemies from behind or sides to eliminate them
3. Avoid enemy vision cones (red triangles)
4. Collect all gems (gold circles)
5. Eliminate all enemies to complete the level
6. If spotted, you lose - tap to retry!

## 🎨 Game Mechanics

- **Player** (green circle) - Your character
- **Enemies** (red circles) - Guards with directional vision
- **Gems** (gold circles) - Collectibles
- **Walls** (brown blocks) - Obstacles and hiding spots
- **Vision Cones** (red transparent) - Enemy detection range
